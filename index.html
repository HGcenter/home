Proprietary Frameworks

HG Center's Proprietary Frameworks

Built from practice, continuously refined

Actively Evolving
F-01
The CEO Triad
三重压力模型

The CEO role structurally generates three emotions: loneliness (feedback distortion from information asymmetry), fear (the most suppressed — disguised as controlling or aggressive behavior), and guilt (someone loses in every decision, a structural feature of the role rather than a moral failure). The three reinforce each other in a closed loop; coaching works by opening each layer in turn.

Use case: 1:1 executive coaching
Entry point: when control-seeking or avoidance becomes visible
F-02
The Deferred Invoice
延迟账单诊断框架

Organizational problems are deferred invoices for leadership failures. Diagnostic path: symptom layer (falling execution, frequent turnover, decision paralysis) → behavior-pattern layer (control, avoidance, people-pleasing) → cognitive blind-spot layer (delegation = losing control / toughness = strength / uncertainty = weakness). Each layer calls for a different intervention.

Use case: tracing organizational problems to their root
Entry point: when the same team issue keeps recurring
F-03
Decision Clarity Under Pressure
高压决策清醒度模型

In crisis moments, leaders slip into one of three cognitive distortions: over-narrowing (fixating on current data, filtering out signal), over-generalizing (one negative signal reads as total failure), and high-intelligence defense (rationalizing away real fear — a pattern distinctive to highly intelligent clients). Coaching identifies the distortion and restores clarity.

Use case: crisis-specific support
Entry point: when decision quality visibly drops
Case Vignettes

Coaching in Practice

All scenarios below are anonymized and stripped of identifying details

Founder · Series B

"I know what to do — I just can't make myself do it."

A Series B founder faced a management-team rebuild after a raise. He could list every correct decision clearly, yet stalled repeatedly on execution. Three months of coaching surfaced the real block: not capability, but a deep fear that delegation equals losing control.

Outcome

Built out the core executive team within 12 months; the CEO stepped back from day-to-day management to focus on strategy.

Executive · Cross-Cultural Setting

"I feel less and less like myself at this company."

A China-region VP at a foreign tech company, caught for years between headquarters and local teams in a matrix organization. Coaching rebuilt his understanding of influence — from managing upward to enabling horizontally, from proving himself to building a network of trust.

Outcome

Promoted to head of Greater China; team-stability metrics improved significantly.

Co-Founder Conflict

"We're best friends and the worst partners."

Two co-founders hit a serious rift ahead of a Series D; every outside advisor suggested a clean split. Coaching brought a neutral third-party lens, helping both sides surface the real stakes behind the conflict — and reach a structural division of responsibilities.

Outcome

The partnership held. They closed the Series D and now run separate business lines.
