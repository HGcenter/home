OUR APPROACH

Address today’s issue.
Build capacity for the next.

We examine how individual judgment, team responsibilities, and organizational conditions shape a decision. Six steps connect judgment, action, and review, with room to revisit what the evidence shows.

Decision value = current results + capacity gained − future costs

A qualitative lens for weighing choices. We consider individual and organizational capacity separately, without calculating a total score.

01

Clarify the issue

Define what needs to be decided, by when, and the known facts, constraints, and important unknowns.

02

Reconsider judgment and roles

Examine familiar patterns and responsibility boundaries. Choose one adjustment you can observe in practice.

03

Choose and act

Compare options and costs. Record your reasoning, the first action, and conditions for continuing or stopping.

04

Put support in place

Match responsibility with the decision authority, information, capability, and resources it requires.

05

Learn from feedback

Agree on when to review. Use evidence to examine results, behavior, and how the organization is working.

06

Manage the consequences

Identify outstanding commitments and the workload left behind, and agree on who will address them.

Plan for feedback and acceptable costs before deciding. In urgent situations, take necessary action first, then examine the judgment and consequences.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

You may also want to know

What happens in an individual conversation?

You bring a real issue. We work through it with listening, clarification, questions, and feedback, using worksheets when helpful. You choose your actions in light of your circumstances. Legal, financial, or other specialist judgments require the relevant professionals.

Can I use the reading and worksheets on my own?

Yes. Public excerpts and worksheets can be read, completed, and printed without booking a conversation. These materials are currently in Chinese. Email us about the full handbooks, a team trial, or internal organizational use.

Who provides the support? Which languages are available?

Our professional team draws on more than 15 years of enterprise management experience, with backgrounds spanning Fortune 500 businesses and technology startups. We work in English and Mandarin. The issue, participants, and arrangements are agreed during the introductory conversation.

How is information handled when an organization commissions the work?

Before starting, we agree with the commissioning organization and participants on goals, roles, confidentiality, and what feedback may be shared. Private conversation content and feedback needed for organizational work are covered by separate agreements; personal disclosures are not automatically passed to the commissioning party.
